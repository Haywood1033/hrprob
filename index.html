How it works
7 signals. One conviction score.
Every batter is evaluated against 7 independent factors. When 4 or more align, that's a signal play — and the data shows that's where the edge lives.
⚔️
Pitch edge
Batter's xwOBA ≥.360 vs pitcher's primary pitch type. The most selective signal.
🎯
Pitcher matchup
Pitcher xERA ≥4.50 or HR/9 ≥1.20 — broadly hittable today.
🏟
Hitter park
Park factor ≥1.05 or career SLG boost ≥20% at this venue.
💨
Wind out
Wind blowing out ≥6mph at an open-air park. Ball carries further.
🔥
Hot streak
1+ HR in last 20 PA. 🔥🔥 scorching = 3+ HR. Model applies +7–15% boost.
🏏
Pull air edge
Pull% ≥40% with FB% ≥35% facing a fly ball pitcher (GB% ≤38%).
🔒
High confidence
200+ PA sample. Statcast numbers are stable and trustworthy.
